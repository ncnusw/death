# Changelog

## 2026-10-21
- **In Their Footsteps** is now locked until a code is shown in class. The published page holds only a lock screen and an encrypted copy of the game (AES-256-GCM, key derived from the code with PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds), so neither the game nor the code can be read from the source. Codes ignore case, spaces and dashes; a device that has unlocked remembers the code until the code changes. The unencrypted game and the locking script live in `_private/`, which is excluded from git.

## 2026-10-20
- Added **In Their Footsteps** (`games/footsteps/`) for Week 7, *Companioning the Bereaved*. One player is the mourner and wanders anywhere for 15 seconds. The path glows, then fades, and the companion retraces it from memory. Scoring rewards the same route, in the same order, at the same pace (pauses count), and the result appears as a full-screen percentage: green 90%+, yellow 80–89%, red below 80%. Afterwards, both paths are shown together for discussion. Four rounds in four original, hand-drawn-in-code places (beach, forest, sunset meadow, snowfield at night): two **easy** rounds (path glows 10 s, with pauses, direction and end marked), then two **hard** rounds (5 s, no markers). Roles swap every round (P1 → P2, P2 → P1). **Solo** mode: a computer mourner walks, and you retrace it. 8BitDo join screen (D-pad, stick or hat), shared keyboard (WASD or arrows), touch (drag a finger to walk). Pauses when the slide is hidden. Three discussion questions at the end. Uses the site's own sounds: `wow.mp3` for a 90%+ retrace, `win.mp3` at the end.

## 2026-10-14
- **The Swing:** tapping the touch buttons quickly no longer zooms the page in on phones (double-tap and pinch zoom are now blocked). If a browser zooms in anyway, a hint appears and a two-finger pinch zooms back out.
- Launch page: updated the AI disclaimer in the footer to note that, despite review, AI-assisted content may contain errors, and to ask readers to check important information against course readings or other reliable sources.
- Added **Technique Draw** (`games/grief-techniques/`) for Week 6. Students read a bilingual case (雅婷 and 志宏, a couple grieving their son), then press **Draw** to get one of 30 grief-therapy technique cards at random (repeats possible, as many draws as they like). Each card shows the full description, who it is for, steps and case example in Chinese and English, a tap-to-choose **Leans toward: LO / RO / Oscillation** with a short 8-bit sound, and the prompt “How would you adapt this technique for Ya-ting and Chih-hung?” Language switch: 中 / 雙語 / EN. Phones get Case and Technique tabs with a Draw bar at the bottom; wide screens show both side by side. Keyboard: Space = draw, 1/2/3 = leans toward. Three discussion questions under the case.
- Case adapted from Hooghe (2022), *The handbook of grief therapies*, ch. 24 (names, places and details changed); each technique card cites its chapter in Neimeyer (Ed., 2016), *Techniques of grief therapy*.
- **Term Duel:** added a second topic, **Models of Grief** (Week 6, 10 terms): grief-work hypothesis, stage models, task model, attachment theory, meaning-making, Dual Process Model, LO and RO coping, oscillation, continuing bonds. Chinese terms checked against Taiwanese sources. Direct link: `games/term-duel/#grief-models`.
- Added **Term Duel** (`games/term-duel/`), a whole-class vocabulary game on each student's own phone. Students play rock-paper-scissors in person; the winner names a term, the loser gives the Chinese or a plain-English explanation, and the winner holds **Answer** to check (Chinese term plus a short explanation, hidden again on release). Tap to tick, hold to untick. Ticks are saved on the phone, per topic, with a Start Over button. The finish screen is bright green so it can be seen across the room. Topic menu, so more word banks can be added later; first bank: **Advance Care Planning** (10 terms, Patient Right to Autonomy Act). Links can go straight to a topic, e.g. `games/term-duel/#acp`. Uses the site's own sounds.
- Launch page intro no longer says every game ends with discussion questions.
- Added **Line Charades** (`games/line-charades/`), adapted from the earlier slide version: two teams take turns over 5 rounds; guessers close their eyes while the acting team reads its secret line (shown with its stanza), then the guesser picks the line from the whole poem. 1 point for the exact line. Adjustable reading and guessing times; 8BitDo join screen, keyboard and touch; pauses when the slide is hidden. Text: Emily Dickinson, Fr479, after Franklin (Harvard UP, 1998), credited on the title screen. Uses only the site's own sounds.
- Added **Ageism Quiz** (`games/ageism-quiz/`): 10 questions on ageism and grief, shuffled each try, with an explanation after every answer. The score screen turns rainbow at 10/10, green at 7–9, and yellow at 6 or below, so scores can be read across a large room. Try Again button, discussion questions, no sounds. Touch, keyboard (1–4, Enter) and 8BitDo.
- Launch page: added a neutral card colour for games that are not about the Dual Process Model, and removed the Loss-Oriented / Restoration-Oriented legend from the header, since not every game uses those ideas.
- Set up the site: launch page (`index.html`) built from `games.json`, with a backup game list so it also opens straight from a folder.
- Added **The Swing** (`games/swing/`), a 1–2 player Dual Process Model game: moved from the old `scripts/` folder, sound path updated to `assets/audio/`, added large touch buttons for tablets and phones and a rotate-to-landscape hint.
- Replaced the sound effects with original 8-bit jingles (`assets/audio/wow.mp3`, `assets/audio/win.mp3`).
