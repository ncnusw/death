ABOUT THESE GAMES

These educational games were developed with the assistance of Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, and have been reviewed and adapted for teaching. Because AI-generated content can contain errors or inaccuracies, some mistakes may remain despite review. Please use the content with care and check important information against course readings or other reliable sources. These games are learning activities, not clinical or assessment tools, and are not a substitute for professional grief support.
