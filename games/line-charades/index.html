LINE CHARADES

“Because I could not stop for Death —”

Emily Dickinson, Fr479 (c. 1862). Text after R. W. Franklin, ed., The Poems of Emily Dickinson (Harvard University Press, 1998).

  1. Two teams take turns. 5 rounds, one turn per team each round.
  2. Guessers close their eyes. The acting team sees a secret line of the poem.
  3. Act the line out, with no words. Your guesser picks the line from the whole poem.
  4. The exact line scores 1 point. A wrong line or time up scores 0.
