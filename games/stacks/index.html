LIGHT IN THE STACKS

書庫裡的光

第八週的課上到一半，你睡著了。醒來時，你站在三年前的學校圖書館。角落裡，坐著那個總是一個人的阿哲。

Halfway through the Week 8 lecture, you doze off and wake up in the campus library, three years ago. In the corner sits Zhe, alone again.

一個人玩：手機、平板或電腦。兩人投影：一人用 8BitDo 或鍵盤操作，一人唸出對話、負責答題。
Solo on a phone, tablet or computer · Duo on a projector: one drives (8BitDo or keyboard), one reads aloud and answers.

這個故事談到喪親、悲傷，也有一段詢問自殺念頭的對話。如果它讓你想起自己的失落，可以先停下來，找信任的人或學校的諮商中心聊聊。

This story is about bereavement and grief, and includes a conversation that asks about suicidal thoughts. If it brings up a loss of your own, it's okay to stop and talk with someone you trust or your campus counseling center.

換了手機？討論之後，輸入老師給的密碼，直接從「回到圖書館」開始。
New device? After the discussion, enter the password from your teacher to start from "Back to the library".

學校、阿哲和所有角色都是虛構的。這是教學用的簡化故事，不能用來診斷任何人。
The campus, Zhe and everyone in the story are fictional. This is a simplified teaching story and cannot be used to diagnose anyone.
