TERM DUEL

Duel your classmates with rock-paper-scissors and tick off every term.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Find a partner and play rock-paper-scissors.
  2. The winner picks any term on the list and says it aloud.
  3. The loser gives the Chinese, or explains it in plain English.
  4. The winner holds Answer to check. Right? You both tick that term. Wrong? The winner tells the answer and nobody ticks.
  5. Find a new partner and repeat until all your boxes are ticked.

Same term again? That's fine. Extra practice helps you remember.
