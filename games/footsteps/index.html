IN THEIR FOOTSTEPS

Walk where the mourner walked: same path, same order, same pace.

Based on the eleven tenets in Wolfelt (2009), The Handbook for Companioning the Mourner.

  1. The mourner wanders for 15 seconds: loop, pause, double back, anything.
  2. The path glows for a while, then fades away.
  3. The companion retraces it from memory. Order and pace count: linger where they lingered.
  4. 4 rounds, 4 places: 2 easy (path glows 10 s, pauses marked), then 2 hard (5 s, no hints). Roles swap every round.
